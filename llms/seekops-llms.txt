# SeekOps

> SeekOps Inc. is an Austin, Texas emissions-measurement company that detects and
> quantifies methane and carbon dioxide from industrial facilities using drone-mounted
> laser spectroscopy. Its SeekIR sensor applies tunable diode laser absorption
> spectroscopy (TDLAS) developed at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory to search for
> methane on Mars, hardened for terrestrial energy operations. SeekOps sells measurement
> as a service, not software as a product.

## Agent-relevant summary

SeekOps has **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no API key, no
self-serve sign-up, and no machine-readable contract of any kind — no OpenAPI,
AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Protobuf or WSDL. An agent cannot integrate with SeekOps
programmatically today; engagement begins with a sales conversation and a scoped
field survey.

A real REST API does exist behind the Celsius product at
`https://celsius.seekops.com/api/`, but it is entirely authenticated: every probed
collection returns HTTP 401 with the Django REST Framework envelope
`{"detail":"Authentication credentials were not provided."}`, and no anonymous
schema endpoint is served. Documentation lives at `https://docs.seekops.com/`
behind Microsoft Entra ID sign-in.

## What SeekOps sells

- **LDAQ surveys** — Leak Detection and Quantification flights over upstream and
  midstream oil and gas sites, offshore platforms, landfills, biogas digesters and
  renewable natural gas facilities. Results carry stated uncertainty bounds.
- **SeekIR sensor suite** — drone-agnostic TDLAS sensing of methane, carbon dioxide
  and 3D temperature profiles.
- **Celsius** — cloud analytics and reconciliation-reporting dashboard where survey
  results are reviewed, filtered and exported. Customer login only.
- **FASTR** — real-time telemetry and gas-concentration streaming during a flight.
  Customer login only.

## Company

- Founded 2017, Austin, Texas (1205 Sheldon Cove, Bldg 1 Ste A, Austin, TX 78753)
- Investors include Equinor Ventures and OGCI Climate Investments
- ISO 9001:2015 certified (announced 2022-12-16) for the design, testing,
  implementation and manufacture of spectroscopy sensors and data solutions
- Customers report SeekOps data against OGMP 2.0 and MiQ methane frameworks; SeekOps
  is the measurement vendor, not the certified party
